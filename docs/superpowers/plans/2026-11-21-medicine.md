**Goal:** Add an educational anatomy camera to the Medicine module with articulated illustrations.
**Architecture:** Separate lazy React module, pose worker, pure SVG coordinate geometry and selectable anatomy catalog. Reuse camera lifecycle, ship local pose assets through existing scripts and PWA.
**Tech Stack:** React, TypeScript, MediaPipe 0.10.32, SVG, Vitest, Playwright.
**Spec:** docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-21-medicine-design.md

## Global Constraints
- Three layers: muscles, bones, organs; one frontal person; local inference only.
- Existing vocabulary, camera and nonmedicine profiles remain available.
- Stop resources on navigation, profile editing, hidden page and failure.

- Mobile controls and accessible selection remain usable.
